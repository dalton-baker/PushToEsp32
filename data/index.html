How do I find my coordinates?
iPhone — Compass app:
  1. Open the Compass app
  2. Coordinates are at the bottom (e.g., "37.7749 N, 122.4194 W")
  3. Tap and hold to copy, then paste below
Android — Google Maps:
  1. Open Google Maps
  2. Long-press anywhere to drop a pin
  3. Coordinates appear in the search bar — tap and copy

Or just type them. For astronomy, ±0.01° (~1km) is plenty.

About 1-star vs 2-star alignment

1 star — level ground: Corrects the encoder zero-point offset. Point at one known star, press Set Star 1. Keep the scope roughly level.

2 stars — any ground: Computes a full 3-D rotation that accounts for mount tilt. Point at a second star and press Set Star 2. No leveling required.
